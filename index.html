Behind the analysis

An operations perspective.
A data-driven approach.

I’m a Warehouse Section Leader with over 12 years of experience in FMCG logistics and warehousing. Alongside leading operations, I produce daily and weekly reports for six senior managers, helping them understand productivity, service performance, stock accuracy and operational risk.

I use Excel and Power Query to reconcile data from four operational sources and investigate trends and exceptions. By automating a recurring reporting process, I removed approximately 1.5 hours of manual preparation per cycle. My analysis has also supported actions that contributed to a 10% improvement in Intake Rate performance.

I’m now looking to move into a dedicated data analyst or operations reporting role. Through my SQL, Power BI and Python projects, I’ve developed my skills in data preparation, modelling and communicating findings, building on the reporting work I already do.

I bring an understanding of what happens on the warehouse floor and how reporting supports labour, capacity and workload decisions. I want to combine that operational experience with analysis that people can use.
